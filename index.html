About Me

Hi, I'm Liam

I am a 19-year-old programmer currently studying Games Programming at Staffordshire University, with a passion for low-level graphics programming.

From a young age, playing games on my dad's PC, I was always curious about how things worked; how you could place monkeys in Bloons Tower Defense 5, or how the world of Minecraft seemed to go on forever. That curiosity drives my projects and fuels my interest in building efficient, scalable systems.

Outside the world of programming, I enjoy gaming, with over 4000 hours on Steam alone, spending most of my time in competitive shooters, factory simulations and the occasional game of pool or darts with friends and family. Additionally, I build PCs (when there isn't a RAM shortage) and love to cook.
